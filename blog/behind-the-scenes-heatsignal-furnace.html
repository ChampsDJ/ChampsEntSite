Behind the scenes: heatsignal's FURNACE

Two floors, one warehouse, and a night of tech house at The Playground.

Published September 2026 · 5 min read

The event

heatsignal's FURNACE was one of the more memorable nights I've played in Pittsburgh. It's an underground rave held at The Playground, and the party spanned two full floors of the building, each with its own sound and its own energy, running for most of the night.

What it takes to play a two-floor warehouse party

Playing a space like this is a different job than a wedding or a club set. There's no fixed timeline to follow and no schedule of speeches or toasts, just a crowd that's there to move, and a set that has to earn its energy in real time.

A few things that matter most in a setting like this:

  • Reading a crowd that's constantly shifting between floors, and pacing the set so the energy builds instead of stalling.
  • Committing to a genre and a sound. This night was tech house, start to finish, and staying true to that vibe rather than chasing every direction the crowd suggests.
  • Trusting the room. An underground crowd knows the genre they came for, and the job is to go deeper into it, not water it down.

The energy

Two floors of music running at once meant the building had its own rhythm all night, people moving between rooms, energy building on one floor while the other cooled off, then trading places later on. It was one of those nights where the crowd and the music built off each other for hours, exactly the kind of set that reminds me why underground and club events are still some of my favorite bookings.
