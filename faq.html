FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

If you have any questions about something that isn't covered in our FAQ, fill out our contact form and we will get back to you ASAP!

WHAT GENRES OF MUSIC DO YOU PLAY?

Our music library spans house, trance, techno, dubstep, drum & bass, hip-hop, rock, Top 40, and beyond. We program each event around your guests and adjust in real time to keep the floor full.

CAN WE GIVE YOU SONGS WE WANT PLAYED?

Of course. Share your must-plays, favorites, and do-not-play list. We’ll build them into a set that feels natural and keeps the momentum up.

DO YOU TAKE REQUESTS FROM GUESTS?

Yes, unless you prefer otherwise. We filter requests through your vision for the night and the energy on the floor.

CAN YOU MIX DIFFERENT STYLES FOR A DIVERSE CROWD?

Yes. Blending generations and genres smoothly is a core part of what we do.

DO YOU PROVIDE SOUND AND LIGHTING?

We arrive with professional-grade audio and can design lighting to match the size and style of your venue.

HOW DO YOU HANDLE ANNOUNCEMENTS AND EVENT FLOW?

We provide polished MC support when needed and can be as visible or as minimal on the microphone as you prefer. Wireless microphones are available for toasts and speeches.

HOW EARLY DO YOU ARRIVE?

Typically 2 to 3 hours before guest arrival. This allows time for setup, testing, and coordination with planners and venue staff.

WHAT HAPPENS IF EQUIPMENT FAILS?

We bring backup systems and test everything before the event begins, ensuring continuous music.

CAN YOU STAY LONGER IF THE PARTY IS GOING STRONG?

If the venue allows it, we can extend for an additional fee. You can decide ahead of time or during the event. If you decide during the event, payment must be made before extra time begins.

WHAT DO YOU WEAR?

We dress to match the occasion. Formal for weddings and corporate events, refined and stylish for parties. Specific requests are always welcome. If you have a dress code, we’ll follow it.

WHAT SECURES OUR DATE?

Your event is confirmed once the contract is signed and the deposit is received. The remaining balance is due 30 days prior.
