EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

YINZERS MEET FAKE WEDDING

Champs provided the entertainment for the Fake Wedding social mixer by Yinzers Meet at Pitch Social on Carson St. on 9/19/25. We acted like we were hosting a wedding reception and had a great time. Two sets below, the first one is more of a "dinner" type of mix while we set up, and then the main event set.

VENANGO PRIDE

Champs participated in Venango Pride in June 2025. It was our first Pride event and we were so excited to be invited to play. This set was nudisco and disco house remixes.

SPIGOLO

Champs played at Spigolo Coffee in Edgewood alongside Mem_ary in May. This little coffee shop was a gem to play at. We stuck to Organic, Melodic, Progressive House vibes for this one.

PITTSBURGH AGAINST APARTHEID

Champs participated in a local fundraiser event in February 2025, which we helped co-produce alongside DJs Against Apartheid and Not On Our Dime Pittsburgh. We were responsible for video & audio recording and the livestream. Champs also played a disco set, which is below.

This video is an excellent demonstration of our current livestream and recording capabilities.

COLOMBINO

Champs played at and produced Pittsburgh's First Coffee Truck Party of 2025 at the Colombino Coffee Truck in Lawrenceville. We provided all equipment, sound, power (we ran the whole event off of a generator battery), and social media marketing, as well as played two sets, both of which are below.
