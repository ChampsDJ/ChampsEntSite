HOW TO BOOK & NEXT STEPS

Booking is easy! To get started, have a look on this page at our recording & livestreaming packages. Then, simply visit our contact page and fill out our form with your event details, including what types of audio sources need to be recorded and what camera angles are important to you.

From there, we will reply to you via email with a custom quote. If you decide to move forward, we will set up an initial consultation meeting. On that meeting, we will ask about your event, how you see it unfolding and how we can assist you in making sure your event is captured with care. The purpose for this initial call is to make sure that we are the right fit for you and your event and understand what we will be recording/streaming!

Shortly after the end of the call, we will provide you with a custom service contract that you can take and review before you get back to us. Once the contract is signed, your date is locked in and we will move forward with the planning phases from there!
