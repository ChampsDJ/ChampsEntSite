HOW TO BOOK & NEXT STEPS

Booking is easy! To get started, have a look on this page at our available equipment. Then, simply visit our contact page and fill out our form with your event rental needs. Please list the equipment you are interested in renting and the duration of your rental.

From there, we will reply to you via email with a custom equipment rental quote from your contact page information.

If the quote terms are agreed upon, we will provide you with a custom rental service contract that you can take and review before you get back to us. Once the contract is signed and your deposit is paid, your rental is locked in and we will go from there!
